Does it work yet?

The MVP gate is one sentence: share a TikTok from TikTok, and end up looking at a correct Dutch recipe in your own library. Three of its four legs are real.

01 Read the source TikTok, Instagram and YouTube via Apify; any blog via JSON-LD, microdata, WP Recipe Maker or plain page text. 6 of your 8 real links produce evidence. works
02 Turn it into a Dutch recipe Verified on a leukerecepten page and on a spoken-English TikTok. 1 lb → 454 g and 1 cup → 240 ml come back marked derived, never explicit. works
03 Save it and read it back Recipe, ingredients and steps land in Postgres; the library and detail screens render them with per-ingredient provenance dots and source attribution. works
04 Do it from inside the app The missing leg. There is no POST /v1/imports and no import screen, so recipes currently get in by running scripts/try_import.py from a terminal. This is M11, M12 and M17. missing
